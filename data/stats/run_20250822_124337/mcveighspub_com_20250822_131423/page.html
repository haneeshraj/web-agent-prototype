About

McVeigh’s

Since 1962, McVeigh’s Irish Pub has been Toronto’s go-to spot for authentic Irish hospitality. With a wide selection of draft beers, fine whiskies, and classic pub fare, we offer more than just great food and drinks—we create unforgettable experiences.
Step inside and feel right at home, surrounded by portraits of Irish poets and vibrant art of bands like The Chieftains. Enjoy the lively spirit of McVeigh’s with authentic Celtic tunes and traditional Irish music on select nights. Whether you’re grabbing a late-night bite or sharing a pint with friends, there’s always a seat waiting for you. Come and experience a night to remember!

Our Offerings

Drinks
With a wide selection of draught beers, including rich stouts, crisp lagers, and smooth ales, there’s always the perfect pour waiting for you. Gather your friends, grab a round, and soak in the warm, welcoming atmosphere—because there’s no better way to enjoy a drink than with friends!
Drink Menu
The Kitchen
At McVeigh’s Classic Irish Pub, we serve up hearty, traditional Irish dishes alongside classic pub fare that keeps you coming back for more. Indulge in our signature Irish stew, savor a plate of perfectly battered fish and chips, or dig into a juicy burger with all the fixings. Whether you’re craving a comforting shepherd’s pie or a simple, satisfying ploughman’s platter, every bite is made with authentic flavors and a touch of Irish hospitality. Come hungry, leave happy, and let us serve you a true taste of Ireland!

Live Music
At McVeigh’s Pub, the spirit of traditional music comes alive with the sounds of Irish, Scottish, English, and East Coast Canadian folk tunes filling the air. Whether you’re a longtime fan or discovering the magic of live music for the first time, our talented bands bring the energy and heart of authentic Celtic and folk traditions to every performance. Grab a pint, find your spot, and let the lively rhythms and soulful melodies turn your night into an unforgettable celebration of music, culture, and good times!
