 mission

The best care for you.

N15にすべてお任せください。


This our motto at N15.  At N15 Hair salon, we specialize in Japanese hair care techniques.  Our stylists specialize in precision cutting, digital perms, straight perms, ombre/sombre and Bayalage hair color.  We apply the latest technological advances from Japan and we pride ourselves in using the best products imported from Japan.

hiroki sugita

owner/director

I started N15 from the ground up in November 2015. I had been a master stylist for 15 years honing my craft in Aoyama, Toyko. In Canada, I had helped open a small salon, which I used as a launching pad for N15.  At N15, my goal is to create a community driven environment that is unpretentious, honest and authentic. I would like to honor my craft in part by training other Japanese-North American stylists in my method. My dream is to build a bridge for other Japanese to follow my path. I have worked with several loyal assistants whom I am happy to have helped start their dream. This is part of my work that I am very proud. I hope when you come into N15, you have an immediate sense of who we are and what we are about. We are a team and as such, we encourage you to find the stylist that suits you the best.
