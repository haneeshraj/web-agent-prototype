Founded at the corner of Yonge and Dundonald in 1985 and purchased by Raffaele Morana in 1988. What began as a humble, neighbourhood Italian restaurant, Caffé Volo evolved into Bar Volo and became the vanguard of craft beer in Ontario. Despite being forced to close the original location in 2016, Volo continued to grow and evolve. Currently, with three locations, a bottle shop, brewery, import agency and an annual cask beer festival, this family-run business will continue to enrich and enliven food and drink culture in Toronto and beyond for years to come! Alla Nostra!
